How SchemeConnect AI Works

Step 1 — Profile Collection:
You fill in your personal details: age, gender, caste, income, education, marks, location, disability status, etc.

Step 2 — AI Analysis (Claude):
Your profile is sent to the Claude AI model along with our database of 30+ real government schemes. Claude performs intelligent eligibility matching — going far beyond keyword search. It understands context, knows which schemes apply to your caste/income/education combination, and explains exactly why each scheme is suitable for you.

Step 3 — Ranked Recommendations:
Claude returns a prioritised list with eligibility reasons, benefits summary, documents required, and direct application links.

Data Sources:

  • myscheme.gov.in (Official Govt of India API)
  • tn.gov.in/schemes.php (Tamil Nadu State Schemes)
  • india.gov.in (Central Featured Schemes)
  • scholarships.gov.in (National Scholarship Portal)

Privacy:
Your API key is stored only in your browser's localStorage. Your profile data is sent to Anthropic's API (same as claude.ai) and not stored anywhere else. No backend server involved.
