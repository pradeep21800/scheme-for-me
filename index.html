About SchemeConnect ML Engine

SchemeConnect uses a two-stage ML pipeline:

Stage 1 – Hard Rule Filter: Eliminates ineligible schemes based on age limits, income caps, caste categories, gender requirements, state restrictions, and education level.

Stage 2 – Weighted Ensemble Scoring:
• TF-IDF Cosine Similarity (30%) – Converts your profile into a text query and matches it against scheme keywords/descriptions
• Rule Score (50%) – How closely you match scheme criteria (marks, income ratio, disability match)
• Tag Boost (20%) – Bonus for exact user-type, caste, and occupation tag matches

Data is sourced from myscheme.gov.in, tn.gov.in, and india.gov.in — refreshed daily via automated scraping.
